I am launching a new YouTube channel called **AI Coding with K**.

The reason is simple: I want a better place to teach how real software products are built. A lot of developer content stops at syntax, libraries or isolated tutorials. Those are useful, but they do not always show the thinking behind a product: why a feature exists, how tradeoffs are made, how code changes when requirements change and how engineering decisions shape the final user experience.

I want the channel to fill that gap.

AI Coding with K is for Android, Kotlin and KMP developers, but it is also for junior developers, students and product minded engineers who want to build useful software with AI without falling into hype. I will talk about AI tools, agentic coding workflows, AI features inside apps, architecture, testing, code review, Jetpack Compose, Kotlin Multiplatform and the product decisions behind the code.

The first few videos will set the tone for the channel. Expect a short channel intro, practical sessions on how I use AI as a software engineer, beginner friendly AI tooling and agent workflow videos, notes on testing AI assisted code and real build sessions where the product and engineering decisions stay visible. Some videos will be polished, but many will be screen led and practical because I want the channel to build a publishing rhythm instead of waiting for everything to feel perfect.

If you are planning to follow the channel, I would love topic suggestions. Tell me what would be useful: Android and Kotlin problems, KMP questions, AI assisted development workflows, app ideas, architecture tradeoffs, testing questions, hackathon preparation or product building topics that are hard to learn from normal tutorials.

My goal is to make the channel practical, calm and useful. Not AI hype. Not only code that works once. I want to use the channel to show how products move from idea to something usable.
